# One experience, two states

Both screens use the same rule sheet. Figma: **Current · Caution screen** section in the [Ocean Water Quality Beacon file](https://www.figma.com/design/J1Pb5bsoBUaovTzt8HiuRx/Ocean-Water-Quality-Beacon).

| | State 1 · Caution | State 2 · No data |
|---|---|---|
| Figma frame | Menu states · at top (static) | Mockup · No data (state 2) |
| Situation | Recent sample, bacteria elevated after rain | Last sample is 6 days old |
| Hero arc | Gradient fill to the middle third, white reading dot | Empty neutral grey track, no fill, no dot |
| Status word | "Caution" in white | "No data" in grey |
| Edge glow | Yellow | None |
| Reason | Bacteria elevated after rain. | No recent water sample for this beach. |
| Advice | Wait 24–48 hours before swimming. | Check posted signs or ask a lifeguard before swimming. |
| Update line | Updated 9:20 AM · Nov 15 | Last sample Nov 10 · 6 days ago |
| Enterococci | 110 MPN/100 mL | — · No recent sample |

## How the rules apply

- **The status stays the largest element** in both states, and it is always a word.
- **No status color appears in No data.** The arc track turns neutral grey instead of showing the faint red-to-green scale, and the glow is removed. Showing even the faint scale would hint at a position on it.
- **The advice stays action-phrased in both.** In No data it points to another source instead of guessing.
- **The update line becomes the key line in No data.** It explains why there is no status.
- **What stays the same:** the menu bar, the beach name, the layout, the forecast notice and the other readings. Rainfall, temperature and waves still have current data.
